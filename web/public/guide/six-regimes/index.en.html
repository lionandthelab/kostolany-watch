Kostolany’s six regimes (A1–B3), explained

Updated 2026-07-31

Kostolany Watch splits the cycle into six stretches. Treat the list below as a learning map, not trade instructions.

Upside (right of the egg)

  • A1 Correction (up) — Just off the bottom; little attention, much bad news already priced in.
  • A2 Accompanying (up) — Trend takes hold and participation spreads.
  • A3 Exaggeration (up) — Overheat and euphoria; risk is easy to underweight when everyone is bullish.

Downside (left of the egg)

  • B1 Correction (down) — Post-peak pullback; optimism lingers while strength fades.
  • B2 Accompanying (down) — Decline spreads and sentiment freezes.
  • B3 Exaggeration (down) — Fear and forced selling; pessimism can crowd the room.

How to read the UI

Colors, names, and probabilities are a current reading, not a gold label. When AI marks disagree, treat agreement as weak.
