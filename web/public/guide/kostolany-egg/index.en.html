What is the Kostolany egg? A simple map of market cycles

Updated 2026-07-31

André Kostolany described markets as an egg-shaped cycle: optimism climbs the right side; pessimism descends the left. Kostolany Watch turns that metaphor into an educational regime-recognition lens.

What the egg is for

The point is not “buy or sell now,” but estimating where crowd, activity, and sentiment sit. After a bottom, attention is scarce; near a peak, almost everyone is bullish. The egg makes that pattern memorable.

Why probabilities

Markets are rarely a single pin. So this site shows regime leanings as probabilities, with three AI marks on the rim rather than one forced answer.

How to use it

  • Before reacting to headlines alone, check where the regime view sits on the egg.
  • Compare US equities (S&P 500) and crypto — rhythms can differ.
  • High confidence is not a trade signal. This is an educational aid.
